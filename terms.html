1. Purpose

Mallang Haru Uploader ("the App") is a personal tool that uploads short videos created by the owner of the Mallang Haru channel to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts.

  • Only original content created by the owner is uploaded. Every draft is reviewed and published manually by the owner in the TikTok app, including any AI-generated content labels.
