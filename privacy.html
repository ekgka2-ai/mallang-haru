1. Who uses this app

Mallang Haru Uploader ("the App") is used only by the owner of the Mallang Haru channel to manage the owner's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

2. Information we access

  • Basic TikTok account information (open ID, display name) through TikTok Login Kit, to confirm the connected account.
  • An access token and refresh token issued by TikTok, used only to upload videos as drafts through the TikTok Content Posting API.

3. How it is used and stored

  • Tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer and are never sent to any other server or third party.
  • Only videos created by the owner are uploaded. The App does not read, collect, or analyze other users' data, comments, or messages.
  • The App does not sell, share, or use data for advertising.

4. Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and permissions). When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the owner's computer.
